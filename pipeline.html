FORMAL MATHEMATICAL MODELS

Mathematical Formulations

Rigorous formulation of feature extraction, graph message passing, and in-silico nucleotide quantization.

∑

1. Vision Transformer Latent Representation

$$z_0 = [x_{class}; x_p^1 E; x_p^2 E; \dots; x_p^N E] + E_{pos}$$ $$z_L = \text{TransformerEncoder}(z_0)$$ $$e_{img} = \frac{z_L^0}{\|z_L^0\|_2} \in \mathbb{R}^{768}$$

The CLS token output from the 12th encoder block is $L_2$-normalized to project onto the unit hypersphere.

2. k-NN Graph Message Passing Diffusion

$$S_{ij} = \frac{e_i \cdot e_j}{\|e_i\| \|e_j\|}, \quad A_{ij} = \max(S_{ij}, 0) \cdot \mathbb{I}(j \in \mathcal{N}_k(i))$$ $$\tilde{A} = \max(A, A^T) + I_N, \quad P = D^{-1} \tilde{A}$$ $$E_{refined} = (1 - \alpha) E + \alpha (P E)$$

A training-free random-walk diffusion smoothing representations along the empirical visual manifold.

3. Deterministic DNA Quantization & Mapping

$$q_d = \text{clip}\left(\left\lfloor \frac{e_d + 1.0}{2.0} \times 255 + 0.5 \right\rfloor, 0, 255\right)$$ $$b_{d, 1..8} = \text{binary}_8(q_d)$$ $$\phi(00) = \text{A}, \; \phi(01) = \text{C}, \; \phi(10) = \text{G}, \; \phi(11) = \text{T}$$

Each 8-bit dimension generates 4 nucleotides, yielding a fixed-length simulated sequence of $768 \times 4 = 3,072$ nucleotides.

RESEARCH ROADMAP

Planned Future Scope (Milestone 2 — 100%)

Components designed for upcoming milestones to achieve full project completion.

◎ Planned for Milestone 2
PLANNED GNN

Trained Inductive Graph Neural Network

Replace the heuristic k-NN diffusion with a trainable Graph Convolutional Network (GCN) or Graph Attention Network (GAT) trained with supervised contrastive loss to learn non-linear relational manifolds.

PLANNED DNA CODEC

Biological Error-Correcting Codes

Incorporate Reed-Solomon or Luby Transform (DNA Fountain) outer codes, homopolymer run-length limits ($< 3$), and GC-content balancing (45%–55%) for biochemical synthesis compatibility.

PLANNED SIMULATION

Noisy In-Silico Sequencing Simulation

Simulate enzymatic PCR amplification and Nanopore/Illumina sequencing error models (substitutions, insertions, deletions) to evaluate sequence retrieval robustness under degradation.

PLANNED BENCHMARK

Large-Scale Retrieval Evaluation

Benchmark mean Average Precision (mAP@k), Recall@k, and NDCG across ImageNet-100 and MS-COCO image subsets to validate empirical gains over unrefined ViT embeddings.
